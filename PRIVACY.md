# Privacy Policy — Muse Notebook

Last updated: November 27, 2026

"Muse Notebook" is a personal application used by its owner with their own Google account. It connects the owner's AI assistant (Muse) to the Gemini Notebook API so the assistant can create notebooks and add sources at the owner's direction.

## Data accessed
Gemini Notebook notebooks and sources (titles, and content the owner adds), through Google's official Gemini Notebook API, using the OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/notebook.

## How data is used
Data is processed only to carry out the owner's own requests. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and no data is used for advertising.

## Retention and deletion
The owner can revoke this app's access at any time from their Google Account's third-party access settings, and can delete notebooks and sources at any time in Gemini Notebook.
